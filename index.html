Not listening

Tap start when you're ready

Running this inside Claude's chat preview can silently block microphone access — you won't get an error, it'll just do nothing. If Start doesn't visibly change to "Listening" within a second or two, open this page directly in your phone's browser instead of the in-chat preview: tap the file card above → the share/external-link icon → open in Chrome. A real browser tab can properly ask you for microphone permission.
Speech is processed live in your browser only to check against your own notes below. Nothing is saved, uploaded, or stored — the moment you stop listening, it's gone. Your notes themselves are saved privately on this device/account so they're here next time.
